# THE OLD ME: Project Brief

## Project

- A standalone, first-person, 18+ psychological and body-horror game, separate from rayanupadhyay.com (the site will only link to it).
- **Platforms:** Windows and macOS. Development happens on a MacBook Pro.
- **Release:** itch.io first, Steam later. Consoles, VR and mobile are maybe-laters.
- **Engine:** not decided yet. Unity HDRP is the leading option, because it runs well on a Mac and can build both the Mac and Windows versions from it. UE5 is still possible on a high-end Apple Silicon MacBook Pro, but it can't package Windows builds from a Mac. Decide once the MacBook's chip, RAM and free storage are known.
- **Scope:** one detailed location (a condemned tower block), about 60–120 minutes, five nights, multiple endings, one final boss fight, plus two earlier fights against Hook.

## Content rules

- Rated 18+ (Mature): blood and gore, intense violence, strong language.
- Heavy gore and body horror are central. Strong language is used naturally in dialogue, not on every line.
- No sexual content or nudity.
- All characters, names and settings are original. No Marvel or other existing characters, names, costumes or lore.
- The claws must not resemble a famous clawed superhero: they grow from bone, curve, there are two per arm, they come out of the underside of the wrist, and nobody heals.
- Never mention Rayan's school, its staff, students or events, anywhere.
- No doctor jokes.
- Include a reduced-gore option in settings.
- Never commit secrets or keys.

## Look and feel

- High-end realistic graphics, far beyond the three.js games on the website.
- **Inspirations (mood and technique only, never copy):** P.T., Resident Evil 7, Visage, Layers of Fear (2023), Silent Hill 2 remake, Amnesia, Alan Wake 2, Still Wakes the Deep, Dead Space (gore and dismemberment feel).
- **Lighting:** handheld lamp or torch as the main light, flickering fluorescents, lightning through broken windows, volumetric rain and dust.
- **Sound** matters as much as visuals: rain, wind through empty floors, creaking concrete, wet foley, claw scrapes, breathing, heartbeat.
- **Setting:** Halden Tower, a condemned 1970s brutalist tower block in a rain-soaked city, today and in 1979.
- Grounded, not supernatural. Strange events come from Hook rigging the building and from Elias's failing body and mind. The player never knows which is which.

## Story

### Premise

Elias Marr, 78, was secretly the vigilante "Hook" for three years in the 1970s. An illegal trial by a private lab called Lattice, meant to fix his bone disease, made two long, curved, bone-white claws grow from the underside of each wrist. He quit in 1979 when his little brother Theo, 15, vanished. He has always believed the gang took Theo to get back at Hook.

Fifty years later the claws are a curse:

- they keep growing and no longer fully retract, so he files them down nightly with a rasp and wraps his wrists in bandages;
- bone spurs push through his forearms and his fingers are stiffening;
- every use cracks them and makes him bleed;
- he never heals.

He is the last tenant of Halden Tower, condemned and due for demolition in seven days. The power flickers, the lift is dead and everyone else has gone. He refuses to leave because it's the flat he and Theo grew up in.

### The truth

Lattice never let him go. They kept his tissue samples and the recordings of his brain from the trial. For decades, in a sealed lab on floor 14 of Halden Tower, they grew clones of him to perfect the claws as a weapon, and imprinted each one with Elias's memories from his Hook years. Most failed. **Subject 9** didn't.

Lattice has abandoned the lab. Subject 9 is the only clone left alive, and he calls himself **Hook**.

Lattice also took Theo in 1979, as a test subject who was a genetic match for Elias. Theo died on floor 14. Hook has known this all along.

### Hook (Subject 9)

- **What he is:** grown to the body of a 22-year-old and imprinted with Elias's memories up to 1979, nothing after.
- **What he believes:** that he's the real Elias, the one who never quit, and the old man is a cowardly impostor living his life.
- **What he wants:** to kill the impostor and take his name before Lattice's cleaners arrive to destroy the evidence, him included.
- **Personality:** evil to the end, with no redemption.
- **Look:** a leather jacket and stitched leather mask, rebuilt from Elias's memories. Lab scars, surgical ports, steel bracing bolted along his forearms to hold the claws, and a subject number tattooed under his jaw.
- **Claws:** the same design as Elias's (bone, curved, two per arm), but perfect and gleaming. He doesn't heal either.
- **Rules:**
  - the sound of claws scraping draws him;
  - he's fast and silent on stairs;
  - he knows the ducts, lift shafts and ceiling voids because he was raised inside the walls.
- **Sample lines:**
  - "You had my life and you wasted it."
  - "Fifty fucking years hiding in my flat, old man."
  - "Say my name. Go on. Say it."
  - "I'm what you'd be if you'd had the balls."

### Voices

- **The intercom:** it's Hook, using Elias's young voice. Later he imitates Theo.
- **Theo's tapes:** cassettes about his day, his debts and how proud he is of his secret-hero brother. The last ones were recorded on floor 14.
- **Lattice logs:** lab files and audio that reveal the cloning programme and what happened to Theo.
- **Scratched notes:** Hook writes them in Elias's own handwriting so Elias thinks he's losing his mind (e.g. "Don't go to 14").

## Synopsis

### Prologue: 1979

Rain over an unfinished tower block. Hook, the city's secret vigilante, chases a gang runner across scaffolding with his claws out, catches him, gets the information and disappears. At home, 22-year-old Elias peels off the mask, and Theo, 15, grins from the doorway. He's the only one who knows. A week later, Theo doesn't come home.

### Night 1: Last Tenant

- Elias, 78, wakes in his flat, with a demolition notice on the door: seven days.
- First interaction: unwrapping the bandages and filing his yellowed, cracked claws in first person. It hurts, and he bleeds.
- "Don't go to 14" is scratched into the mirror in his own handwriting.
- Gore beat: clearing a blocked sink, he pulls out a knot of hair with a chipped teenage tooth in it.
- The intercom buzzes at 2 a.m.: "Elias Marr? That's funny. So am I."
- End: a light comes on on floor 14, which has been sealed for forty years.

### Night 2: Someone Else Is Here

- The stairwells don't match his memory: bricked-up doors, missing landings, lights dying exactly when he reaches them.
- The claws as a tool: pry doors and cut boarding. Each use cracks them, makes him bleed and leaves a lasting blur on screen, and the scraping draws Hook.
- First sighting: a masked figure at the end of a corridor, head tilted, claws out.
- Hiding: under beds, in the rubbish chute, behind fridges, with a short hold-breath mechanic (he's old).
- Gore beat: in flat 17C, a bathtub of rust-red water drains to show a failed clone, an old face on a young body with malformed claws, tagged "LATTICE / MARR / SUBJECT 4".
- **First fight:** Hook toys with Elias, cuts him and leaves. "Not yet, old man. I want you to understand first."

### Night 3: The Imprint

- Touching certain objects (a cassette, the burnt remains of his old mask, a gang patch) throws Elias into his playable 1979 memories as young Hook. Then Hook starts appearing inside the memories, in places he was never there.
- The first Lattice logs reveal the cloning programme and Subject 9.
- Gore beat: trapped in a stalled lift, Elias watches through the gap as Hook drags a body down the corridor and carves the same marks that keep appearing on Elias's walls. Hook leans in: "Fifty fucking years hiding in my flat. You had my life and you wasted it."

### Night 4: The Hunter

- The longest night. Hook stops playing and hunts him through ducts, ceiling voids and the dead lift shaft, with proper chases.
- One of Elias's claws snaps mid-use and he has to pull the broken stump out of his own wrist. This is the hardest body-horror scene in the game.
- Gore beat: the growing room on floor 13, full of tanks of failed versions of Elias at every age, some still twitching.
- Key discovery: Theo's last tape, recorded in the lab: "They said Eli sent me. They said it'd fix my bones like it fixed his. I'm not scared. He's Hook. He'll find me."
- The gang never took Theo. Lattice did.
- The intercom, in Theo's voice: "Eli? I'm on fourteen. Come and get me." It's Hook.
- **Second fight:** a beating Elias barely survives.

### Night 5: Floor 14 and the final fight

The sealed lab: cracked steel tables, rotting restraints, rain blowing through a missing wall over the city below. A brutal, close, gory fight between an old man on failing claws and his perfect copy.

- **Phase 1, survive:**
  - Hook is too fast to hit, so Elias uses the room: swinging equipment, collapsing shelves, the open edge of the floor.
  - Every hit shows on Elias's body (gashes, a torn ear), and his claws chip with each block.
- **Phase 2, fight dirty:**
  - Elias drops a pallet of concrete blocks on Hook and drives his claws in.
  - Hook tears off one of Elias's claws and stabs him through the shoulder with it.
  - Elias rips away Hook's jacket, tears a steel brace off his arm and uses it as a weapon.
- **Phase 3, breaking point:**
  - Both are wrecked: Elias half-blind and bleeding out, Hook missing an arm.
  - The fight reaches the sealed room where Theo died. Elias pins Hook to the door and tears off the mask: Elias's own young face, with "9" tattooed under the jaw.
  - Hook laughs through the blood: "I'm what you'd be if you'd had the balls."

### The final choice

There is no hidden meter. The player chooses the ending in one last-minute choice at the end of the fight, and nothing earlier changes which ending they get, except that the secret ending also needs every tape.

### Endings

1. **Remember (true):** Elias kills Hook and opens the door. Inside are Theo's remains, his cassette recorder still in his pocket. Elias calls the police with the Lattice files: "Floor fourteen. They took my brother. Come and see." He dies against the wall at dawn with the recorder in his ruined hands. Press play: Theo laughs.
2. **Replaced (bad):** Hook wins and walks out in Elias's coat. A black van is waiting: "Welcome back, Subject 9."
3. **Mercy (dark):** Elias lowers his claws. Hook sits beside him, puts his own away, then kills him anyway.
4. **Run:** Elias escapes before the end. On demolition day the tower comes down, and a figure walks out of the dust.
5. **Secret (every tape plus the true ending):** after the credits, a sunny rooftop in 1979. Young Elias shows Theo how the claws work. Theo: "You're the best thing I've got, you know. Even if you're a bit of a freak."

- The website gets an age-gated game page with a download button, but the files are hosted on itch.io. They're too big for GitHub Pages.
